## Persona → need → design response

### Alégor — primary, the social styler

Source: persona panel at y≈5510–6151 (`persona-alegor.webp`, original `image-28.jpeg`); priority map y≈8687–9238.

- Age 21; university student and fashion micro-influencer.
- Wants distinctive pieces that still fit his existing wardrobe, to discover a new drop early, and to know the fit before buying.
- Discovers brands through new drops on TikTok/Instagram, creators and friends. Posts outfits weekly in the scenario.
- Approximately S$100 monthly clothing budget; one S$45–75 top is a treat. This is a persona assumption, not measured customer spending.
- Frictions: product photos fail to show proportions/fit; unisex sizing feels vague; novelty matters.
- Central question: **“What’s new, and will it look good on my feed?”** This is persona scenario copy, not a participant quotation.
- Design responses: homepage Drop 03 + New Arrival rail; Shop All/New In led by the unisex Air Shirt (S$59); linked Lookbook; model measurements and fit notes; simple mobile navigation; 15% first-piece offer reducing price to approximately S$50.
- Proposed journey: Instagram Reel → Home/new drop → Shop All/Air Shirt → product and size guide → 15% offer → Shopee → outfit post and next-drop email.
- Emotional tension, useful synthesis: **He wants to stand out, but cannot afford to guess the fit.** This is a paraphrased design interpretation, not a research quote.

### Diniyan — secondary, the detail-driven buyer

Source: persona panel at y≈6501–7140 (`persona-diniyan.webp`, original `image-23.jpeg`).

- Age 26; marketing executive in a casual CBD office.
- Wants work outfits that survive the heat, enough detail to judge quality/value herself, and trust in an unfamiliar brand.
- Finds brands through Google searches/guides, then compares brands.
- Frictions: vague “premium quality” claims; missing fabric/finishing/care information; unclear delivery and exchange details.
- Central question: **“Is this worth the price, and can I trust the brand?”** Persona copy, not an interview quotation.
- Design responses: useful Journal guides; Women catalogue led by Off-Shoulder Tie Top (S$42); fabric/care/model-size details; size guide; visible FAQ and 30-day returns; marketplace checkout.
- Proposed journey: Google → Journal → Women → product + FAQ → 15% sign-up → Lazada.
- Useful synthesis: **She likes the design, but needs proof before paying.**

### Andrew — tertiary, the community explorer

Source: persona panel at y≈7490–8151 (`persona-andrew.webp`, original `image-24.jpeg`).

- Age 23; final-year student and freelance photographer.
- Mixes thrifted finds with independent local labels and buys only a few pieces a year, carefully.
- Finds brands through friends’ posts, gigs, art markets and indie pop-ups.
- Wants a brand community he identifies with, like-minded people and looks that fit his style.
- Frictions: generic brand stories; “community” reduced to sales posts; not seeing pieces on real people.
- Central question: **“Does this brand have a scene I want to join?”** Persona copy, not observed dialogue.
- Design responses: real-outfit Lookbook; community images used with permission; monthly prompts; Journal and #DressedForTheHeat; Men catalogue led by Stripe Knit Polo (S$55).
- Proposed journey: friend’s post → Community → Lookbook → Men/Polo + size guide → Shopee → own outfit post.
- Useful synthesis: **He wants to belong to a scene, not just buy its merchandise.**

## Other supported decisions

- Research-response panel y≈10968–11489: sweaty MRT walk → fabric/airflow explanations; cold offices/lecture theatres → Suede Zip Jacket as aircon layer + monsoon article; sizing uncertainty → size tabs/fit notes/quick-size control; price-only marketplace competition → explain story/fabric before outbound purchase; trusted checkout → marketplace links with UTM tracking planned.
- Sitemap: **19 → 15 main pages**, plus 12 product pages and six journal articles. Four thin pages were merged: Style Guide into Lookbook; Sustainability into Fabric; Shipping & Returns into FAQ; New Arrivals into Shop sorted New In. Deck goal: at most two clicks from Home, recurring 15% CTA band. This is a design structure/goal, not a measured usability result.
- Visual system: monochrome fashion catalogue, product photos take priority; Archivo for logo/headlines, Inter for products/prices/buttons, Work Sans for body. Case-study shell can inherit portfolio styling while embedded product screens preserve this distinct brand.
- Navigation: shared header/mega menu/footer; desktop hover/mobile tap; mobile full-height sheet and product sticky marketplace CTA; breadcrumbs and category/fit/sort controls.
- Four content pillars: Inspire (Alégor), Explain (Diniyan), Connect (Andrew), Convert (all).
- Copy: calm/confident/weather-aware; short sentences; concrete places and numbers; illustrative fabric charts should be labelled as such.
- Six journal concepts link to products: university outfits→Rose Wash Tee; Southeast Asia packing→Breeze Pant; washing linen→Air Shirt; SG office smart casual→Stripe Knit Polo; hawker lunch→Air Shirt; monsoon→Suede Zip Jacket.
- Community plan: permission for featured outfit photos, monthly themes, S$15 featured-post voucher. State as proposed design/content strategy unless current site implementation is verified.
- Marketing plan: November 11.11 launch, December 12.12/holiday drop, January repeat purchases. **+20% marketplace sales is a target, not an achieved result**. Click/sign-up/redemption targets were to be set after baseline data. No performance gains should be claimed.
- Conversion/PDPA panel documents intended consent/privacy design. Describe visible design choices such as optional marketing consent and a nearby privacy notice; do not certify legal compliance, storage security or operational voucher fulfilment from a slide.
